---
title: 13. Shameless
---

To be in favor or disgrace
is to live in fear.
To take the body seriously
is to admit one can suffer.

What does that mean,
to be in favor of or disgrace
is to live in fear?
Favor debases:
we fear to lose it,
fear to win it.
So to be in favor or disgrace
is to live in fear.

What does that mean,
to take the body seriously
is to admit one can suffer?
I suffer because I'm a body;
if I weren't a body.
how could I suffer?

So people who set their bodily good
before the public good
can be entrusted with the commonwealth,
and people who treated the body politic
as gently as their own body
would be worthy to govern the commonwealth.

> Lao Tzu, a mystic, demystifies political power.
>
> Autocracy and oligarchy foster the beliefs that power is gained magically and retained by sacrifice, and that powerful people are genuinely superior to the powerless.
>
> Lao Tzu does noe see political power as magic. He sees right rightful power as earned and wrongful power as usurped. He does not see power as virtue, but ads the result of virtue. The democracies are founded on that view.
>
> He sees sacrifice of self or others as a corruption of power, and power as available to anybody who follows the Way. This is a radically subservient attitude. No wonder anarchists and Taoists make good friends.
